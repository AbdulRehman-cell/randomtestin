Frequently Asked Questions

Got a question about our workspace etiquette, rules, or community events? We've gathered everything you need to know below.

Do you allow remote work and laptops? ➕
Yes, absolutely! We love supporting Brooklyn's creative minds and remote workforce. We offer unthrottled 500Mbps fiber-optic Wi-Fi, and we have power outlets installed beneath every built-in banquette seat so you can stay fully powered all day. We just ask that you stay mindful of sharing larger table spaces during Peak Lunch Hours (11:30 AM – 1:30 PM).
What are your guidelines for phone calls or meetings? ➕
We ask that you use headphones for listening to audio and take short, quiet voice calls at your seat. If you need to participate in an extended video meeting or standard voice call, please step out onto our garden patio or reserve our private 10-person workspace block in advance to preserve the quiet focus of other guests.
Are dogs allowed in the space? ➕
While NYC Health Department regulations prohibit four-legged friends (except service dogs) inside our food prep area, we have a beautifully sun-drenched outdoor patio space complete with water bowls and shade umbrellas where pets are warmly welcomed!
Do you offer gluten-free and vegan-friendly options? ➕
Definitely. We pride ourselves on creating an inclusive menu. We always offer at least three gluten-free baked goods daily (our Cardamom Almond Muffin is a guest favorite) along with multiple vegan-friendly items across both our pastry case and our sustaining lunch bowls. Please consult our online Menu page to view active dietary tags.
